About GeoIntellis

GeoIntellis is a spatial intelligence technology company building the computational foundation for next-generation underground digital operations.

We develop advanced grid-based digital twin infrastructure that transforms traditional geometry-centric mining workflows into structured, AI-ready spatial systems. Our mission is to enable automated modelling, real-time data fusion, and predictive decision support for complex subsurface environments.

Our Vision

Underground industries face increasing depth, geological uncertainty, and operational complexity. While digital transformation initiatives are accelerating, most digital twins remain static visual representations—fragmented from monitoring systems, simulation models, and predictive analytics.

GeoIntellis aims to bridge this gap by providing a unified spatial intelligence backbone that makes digital twins dynamic, computation-ready, and interoperable.

Our Technology

At the core of GeoIntellis is the GEVF™ (Grid Everything Visual-Fusion) Intelligence Engine—a scalable, topology-aware spatial architecture designed to:

  • Convert CAD layouts into structured grid-based models
  • Automate mining method digital twin generation
  • Enable rapid parametric modelling
  • Integrate real-time monitoring data
  • Support surrogate modelling and geohazard prediction
  • Deliver 3D visual analytics for operational decision-making

Unlike conventional visualisation tools, our platform creates a standardised computational substrate that supports bidirectional interaction between design, data, analytics, and operations.

What We Do

GeoIntellis delivers automated mining digital twin solutions for underground operations, enabling:

  • Faster model construction
  • Agile design iteration
  • Structured multimodal data fusion
  • AI-enabled risk intelligence

Our initial focus is deep underground mining, where complex mining methods and elevated geohazard risks demand scalable digital infrastructure. Over time, our spatial intelligence framework extends to tunnelling, underground infrastructure, and energy storage systems.

Research-Driven, Industry-Focused

GeoIntellis is founded on extensive research in data-driven visualisation, multimodal data fusion, immersive environments, surrogate modelling, and AI-based spatial analytics. Our work has contributed to international journals, patented technologies, and cross-domain applications spanning mining, infrastructure, and environmental simulation.

We combine scientific rigour with engineering practicality to deliver deployable, industry-ready solutions.

Our Ambition

GeoIntellis is building more than a digital twin platform—we are establishing the spatial operating system for intelligent underground systems.

By standardising how subsurface environments are modelled, computed, and analysed, we enable a future where underground decision-making is predictive, adaptive, and data-driven.
